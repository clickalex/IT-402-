🎯 Why it matters

3 marks = usually 2 objective + 1 short answer. Stress + SMART goals asked almost every year.

📖 Concepts (deep)

1. Self-management = Managing yourself — emotions, time, goals, habits — to be productive and balanced. Three pillars: self-awareness (know strengths/weaknesses/values), self-regulation (control reactions), self-motivation (inner drive).

2. Stress: Body/mind pressure from demanding situations. Small stress motivates; excess harms.

  • Causes: Exams/deadlines, overload, change (new school), conflicts, noise/heat, poor sleep, social media pressure.
  • Effects: Headache, insomnia, fast heartbeat, anxiety, anger, poor concentration, low marks.
  • Management techniques (learn any 4 with 1 line):
    1. Time management (plan, prioritise, to-do list)
    2. Exercise/yoga/meditation + deep breathing
    3. 7–8 hrs sleep + healthy food + less junk
    4. Talk to family/friends/teacher (share feelings)
    5. Short breaks + hobbies/music/walk
    6. Positive thinking + realistic goals (don't compare)
    7. Say NO politely to overload

3. Goal setting – SMART (expand + example):

M MeasurableCan count it“20 MCQs + 2 long answers”
A AchievablePossible2 hrs/day, not 10 hrs suddenly

T Time-boundDeadline“By Sunday 6 pm”

4. Time management: To-do list daily → prioritise (Urgent+Important first) → fix time slots → avoid procrastination (“do 25-min pomodoro, then 5-min break”) → review at night. Benefits: less stress, better quality, meets deadlines.

5. Personality (Big Five – OCEAN): Openness (creative/curious), Conscientiousness (disciplined), Extraversion (social/talkative), Agreeableness (helpful/kind), Neuroticism (anxious/moody). No good/bad — awareness helps choose roles (e.g. extravert → sales, conscientious → data entry).

6. Grooming & hygiene: Bath daily, neat uniform/hair/nails, clean shoes, good posture, polite body language. Builds confidence + first impression for interviews/jobs.

7. Self-motivation builders: Positive self-talk (“I can improve 5 marks”), reward small wins, inspiring company, track progress chart, learn from failure (analyse, don't quit).

🧠 Memory tricks

  • SMART → “Smart Monkeys Achieve Really Tough-goals”
  • OCEAN = Big Five order itself. Stress-busters → “TEB-Talk-Break”: Time-plan, Exercise, Balanced-sleep/food, Talk, Breaks.

⚠️ Mistakes to avoid

  • Writing “SMART = good goals” without expanding each letter — zero marks. Always expand all 5.
  • Only listing stress effects without techniques when Q asks “suggest measures”.

❓ Exam Q&A

1-mark:
“T in SMART?” → Time-bound. “Big Five trait for disciplined person?” → Conscientiousness.
2-mark Q:
Maya gets frustrated, reacts impulsively. Suggest two techniques. → (1) Pause + deep breathing/count 10 before reacting (self-regulation). (2) Prioritise with to-do list + short breaks to cut overload.
2-mark Q:
Two benefits of time management. → Finishes work on time with less stress; improves quality + leaves time for revision/hobbies.

💻 Practical

Make a 1-week SMART study timetable in Calc (Goal / Measurable target / Deadline columns) + tick daily. Paste in file as self-management evidence.

➕ Extra exam Q&A bank

1-mark: Expand SMART with one example.
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound — e.g. “Finish DBMS Ch8–9 + 20 MCQs by Sunday 6 pm”.
1-mark: Name the Big Five (OCEAN) traits.
Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism.
1-mark: State one physical and one mental effect of unmanaged stress.
Physical: headache / poor sleep. Mental: anxiety / poor concentration.
2-mark: Two benefits of goal setting for a student.
(1) Gives clear direction and measurable targets, so effort stays focused. (2) Builds motivation and time control — small wins tracked daily reduce exam stress.
4-mark (practice): You have 10 days before the IT exam and feel stressed. Write a self-management plan.
(1) Set SMART targets: e.g. Unit 3 in 4 days, 20 MCQs/day. (2) Make a daily to-do list; prioritise urgent + important first; use 25-min study + 5-min break cycles. (3) Manage stress: 7–8 hrs sleep, exercise/yoga, short walks, talk to family. (4) Track progress on a chart and reward small wins; say no politely to distractions. Review nightly and adjust — planned work finishes on time with less stress.
