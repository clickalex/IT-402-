Chapter 13: Health, Safety and Security

🎯 Marks lens: Hazards, prevention, cyber safety and reporting.

📖 Concepts explained simply

A hazard can cause harm; risk combines likelihood and consequence. Physical safety protects bodies, information security protects data.

Chapter 14: Workplace Quality and Ergonomics

🎯 Marks lens: Posture, quality checks and 20-20-20.

📖 Concepts explained simply

Ergonomics fits the task to the worker. Keep screen about 50–70 cm away and follow 20-20-20.

TermSimple meaningExample
Core ideaA reliable way to organise information and work.A school register or office customer list
Good practiceConsistent names, checked output and saved versions.IT402_Name_Task_v2
Real-life example: A data-entry operator uses this skill to make a record accurate, searchable and easy for a colleague to review.

🖱️ Exact LibreOffice steps

  1. Place screen near eye level; keep back supported and feet flat.
  2. Keep keyboard/mouse close and wrists neutral.
  3. Every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Chapter 15: Accidents and Emergencies

🎯 Marks lens: Electrical/fire actions, evacuation and 101/102/108.

📖 Concepts explained simply

Stay calm, protect yourself, alert responsible people, give location and call the correct service.

TermSimple meaningExample
Core ideaA reliable way to organise information and work.A school register or office customer list

🖱️ Exact LibreOffice steps

  1. Isolate power only if safe; never touch a person on live electricity.
  2. Call 101 police, 102 ambulance or 108 emergency response as taught; state location.
  3. Evacuate, assemble and report missing people; do not return for belongings.
