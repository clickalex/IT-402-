Chapter 5: Green Skills-II

Work productively while protecting the environment — sustainable development, green ICT habits and safe e-waste handling.

📖 Part 1 · Detailed notes❓ Part 2 · Questions💻 Part 3 · Practice
After this chapter you can:
  • Define sustainable development and its three pillars.
  • Explain your role in protecting the environment at school/work.
  • Apply green ICT habits: save power, reduce paper, reuse.
  • Handle e-waste only through authorised channels.
On this page: Details Questions Practice Checklist

📖 Part 1 · Detailed chapter

🎯 Marks lens (1 mark): definition of sustainable development, 3 pillars, e-waste meaning + disposal, 2 green ICT practices. Short but scoring — learn exact terms.

1. Sustainable development

Sustainable development = meeting present needs without reducing future generations' ability to meet theirs. It balances three pillars:

🌍 Environment
air, water, energy👥 Society
health, equity💰 Economy
jobs, growth

Examples: saving electricity lowers bills and pollution; repairing a keyboard saves money and waste.

2. Our role in sustainable development

WhereGreen actions
Computer labPower-saving mode; shut down + switch off monitors; print preview before printing
ClassroomDigital review/PDF comments; duplex final copy only; reuse one-side paper for rough work
HomeUnplug chargers; carry cloth bag; segregate dry/wet waste
CommunityPlant care, e-waste collection drives, spread awareness

3. Green ICT: the 4 Rs + power habits

RMeansICT example
ReduceUse lessProofread on screen; duplex printing
ReuseUse againRefurbish keyboard/mouse; reuse folders
RepairFix before replacingReplace cable, not the whole printer
RecycleRecover material safelyAuthorised e-waste centre only
  1. Enable power-saving/sleep settings on lab computers.
  2. Review digitally: PDF + comments instead of draft printouts.
  3. Print smart: preview → duplex → only the final copy.
  4. Shut down after work; switch off monitors and projectors.

4. E-waste: meaning + safe disposal

E-waste = discarded electrical/electronic equipment and parts: old keyboards, monitors, batteries, cables, bulbs, phones.

❌ Never do thisThrow batteries/electronics in general dustbins; burn cables; dump in open ground.
✅ Correct methodSeparate e-waste; label a collection box; hand over to the school's authorised recycler; keep a handover record.

Why authorised? E-waste contains hazardous substances (lead, mercury) and valuable metals — both need safe, professional recovery.

🧠 Memory trick: Reduce → Reuse → Repair → Recycle. Power: Save → Sleep → Shut down.
⚠️ Common mistakes: "digital work = zero impact" (electricity still counts); printing drafts repeatedly; batteries in general waste; forgetting print preview.

One-mark questions

1. What is sustainable development?
Meeting present needs without reducing future generations' ability to meet theirs.
2. Name the three pillars.
Environment, society and economy.
3. What is e-waste?
Discarded electrical/electronic equipment and components (keyboards, batteries, monitors).
4. Give one green ICT practice.
Power down after work / digital review / duplex printing (any one).
5. Expand the 4 Rs.
Reduce, Reuse, Repair, Recycle.
6. Why not put batteries in general waste?
They contain hazardous substances needing safe authorised recovery.

Two-mark questions

1. Give two green lab practices.
Power-saving + shutdown after work; PDF/digital review instead of draft prints.
2. Why must e-waste go to authorised centres?
It has hazardous substances and valuable materials needing safe professional recovery.
3. Reduce vs reuse — one ICT example each?
Reduce: proofread on screen. Reuse: refurbish a working keyboard.
4. How does duplex printing help?
Halves paper use for the same content, cutting cost and waste.

Four-mark model answer

Explain a green computer-lab plan. (4)
Enable power-saving; shut down + switch off monitors after work. Review drafts digitally (PDF/comments); use print preview and duplex only for final copies. Repair/reuse equipment where safe; collect batteries and electronics separately and hand over to an authorised recycler with a record. Review paper/power savings monthly.

💻 Part 3 · Practice

  1. Lab audit: count how many PCs/monitors/printers stay ON after hours; note wastage.
  2. Green checklist: write a 5-point card for your lab (save/sleep/shutdown/preview/duplex).
  3. E-waste map: find your school's e-waste box/recycler; note what goes in it.
  4. Paper diary: for one week record every print: needed? previewed? duplex? Could it be PDF?
