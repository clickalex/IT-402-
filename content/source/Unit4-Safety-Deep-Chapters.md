# Chapter 13: Health, Safety and Security at Workplace

## 🎯 Marks lens
3–4 marks. Policy reasons + hazard types with examples.

## 📖 Deep concepts

**1. Three words (define separately — examiner checks):**
- **Health** = physical + mental + social well-being (no illness/stress), e.g. eye care, posture, clean air.
- **Safety** = protection from accidents/injury (slips, shocks, fire).
- **Security** = protection of people + data + property from theft/misuse (locks, passwords, CCTV, backups).

**2. Policies & Procedures (what a good company writes down):**
Emergency exits + evacuation map displayed • Fire alarm/drill schedule • First-aid kit location + trained first-aider • Hazard reporting register (who to tell) • Electrical safety rules • Password/backup policy • PPE list (gloves, masks where needed) • Training calendar for new staff.
*Procedure = step-order; Policy = the rule itself.* (Evacuation PROCEDURE follows Safety POLICY.)

**3. Reasons for HSS programs (learn 6):**
1. **Legal** – law demands it (Factories Act, IT rules); avoids fines/closure
2. **Fewer accidents/illness** – healthy staff, less leave
3. **Higher productivity** – safe worker = focused worker
4. **Lower costs** – less medical/compensation/damage bills
5. **Motivation + retention** – staff feel cared, stay longer
6. **Good image** – clients trust a safe, certified workplace

**4. Hazard gallery (THE table — with 2 examples each):**
| Hazard | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | Energy/substance harming body | Loud noise, poor lighting, heat/cold, radiation, slippery floor |
| **Fall from height** | Drop from raised place | Ladder without grip, stair with no railing, loft storage |
| **Slip & Trip** | Lose footing | Wet/oily floor (slip), loose wires/clutter/uneven tiles (trip) |
| **Electrical** | Shock/fire from power | Overloaded socket, cut wire, no earthing, water near switch |
| **Fire** | Burns + smoke damage | Paper near heater, blocked exit, expired extinguisher, gas leak |
| **Health** | Illness over time | Dust/fumes → asthma; long sitting → back pain; screen → eye strain |
| **Chemical** (bonus) | Burns/poison | Printer toner, cleaning acid, lab solvents |

**5. Potential sources of hazards in an organisation:** Unsafe equipment (no guards) • Untrained staff • Poor housekeeping (clutter, spills) • Ignored rules (no PPE) • Overwork/fatigue • Bad design (dark stairs, narrow exits) • No maintenance (frayed wires, shaky shelves).

**6. Hazards using computers (6):** Eye strain • Neck/back/wrist pain (posture/repetition) • Electric shock (open UPS/CPU, spills) • Data loss (no backup/virus) • Stress (deadlines, night shifts) • E-waste (old batteries polluting).

**7. Handling office equipment (rules):** Train first → read manual → switch OFF + unplug before cleaning/moving → no liquids on/near devices → one plug per socket (no overloading) → report sparks/smell/damage at once → AMC/service only by technician.

**8. Handling objects (lifting rules):** Assess weight → bend KNEES (not back), straight back, firm grip → keep load close to body → no twisting (turn feet) → heavy/awkward = ask help or trolley → gloves for sharp/hot • Store heavy at waist height, light on top.

**9. Stress at work:** Causes — deadlines, long hours, conflicts, insecurity, noise. Signs — headache, anger, insomnia, mistakes. Controls — realistic workload, breaks, exercise, counselling, appreciation, clear roles.

**10. Working environment (ideal checklist):** Clean + ventilated + well-lit • 22–26°C comfort • Low noise • Ergonomic furniture • Safe wiring • Clear exits • Drinking water + washrooms • First-aid + extinguisher visible.

**11. Hazard control hierarchy (5 levels —itus):** 1. **Eliminate** (remove risk: replace faulty heater) 2. **Substitute** (safer alternative: LED, water-based ink) 3. **Engineering** (guards, rails, covers, ventilation) 4. **Administrative** (rules, training, signs, shifts) 5. **PPE** (last shield: gloves, mask, specs). Review regularly via audits.

**12. Safety Guidelines Checklist (office walk-through):** ☐ Exits clear + signed + lit ☐ Extinguisher charged + staff know PASS ☐ First-aid stocked + aider named ☐ Wires covered + MCBs labelled ☐ Backup done + antivirus on ☐ Passwords strong + screens lock ☐ Drill practised this quarter ☐ Incident register maintained.

## ❓ Exam Q&A
**1-mark:** “Wet floor = ?” → Slip hazard. “Loose wire on path = ?” → Trip. “Bend knees rule for?” → Lifting.
**2-mark:** *Any 2 reasons for HSS policy.* → Legal compliance + fewer accidents (any 2 with half-line each).
**4-mark:** *Hazard types with examples.* → 5–6 rows of gallery table.

# Chapter 14: Workplace Quality Measures

## 🎯 Marks lens
3 marks. Ergonomics + eye/back remedies + cautions = short-answer gold.

## 📖 Deep concepts

**1. Air quality:** Ventilate (windows/exhaust), no indoor smoking, service AC + clean filters, indoor plants, control dust (vacuum, mats), test CO₂/particulates yearly, separate printer room (ozone/toner).
**Clean-air guidelines:** Fresh-air intake, no blocked vents, pest-free ducts, masks in dusty work.

**2. Water quality:** Purified drinking water (RO/UV tested), coolers cleaned, no seepage/leakage, washrooms disinfected, waste bins covered. Test TDS/bacteria periodically; display “Drinking Water” labels.

**3. Cleanliness (daily routine):** Sweep/mop, dust desks, empty + **segregate bins** (green wet / blue dry / red e-waste + biomedical), washrooms on roster, pest control monthly, “clean desk” habit (files back, food only in pantry).

**4. Office Ergonomics (THE diagram — learn numbers!):**
- Chair: straight back + lumbar support, adjustable height, armrests; feet FLAT on floor/footrest, knees ~90°.
- Desk: keyboard + mouse at ELBOW height, wrists straight (pad helps).
- Monitor: top at/below EYE level, **50–70 cm away** (~arm's length), slight tilt back, no glare (perpendicular to window).
- Laptop: use **stand + external keyboard/mouse** for long hours.
- Habit: **micro-break every 30–60 min** — stand, roll shoulders, walk, stretch; 20-20-20 for eyes.
*Ergonomics definition (1 line): “Designing workplace/tools to fit the worker's body for comfort + efficiency + injury prevention.”*

**5. Computer health & safety tips (8):** Right posture (above) • 20-20-20 rule • Blink often + correct specs • Brightness ~ room light, clean screen • Document holder beside monitor (no neck twist) • No eating over keyboard (crumbs + spills) • Hourly stretch + water • Shut down + cover after work.

**6. Problem → Remedy clinic (learn as pairs — 4-marker ready):**
| Problem | Cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| **Occupational Overuse Syndrome / RSI** (wrist/finger pain) | Repeat typing/clicking | Ergonomic keyboard/mouse, wrist rest, breaks, stretch, correct height |
| **Leg/feet strain** | Long standing | Footrest, sit breaks, supportive footwear, anti-fatigue mat |
| **Eye strain** | Glare, long screen, low resolution | 20-20-20, anti-glare filter, 50–70 cm, bigger fonts, room lighting |
| **Headache** | Stress + posture + dehydration + glare | Breaks, water, posture fix, screen height, eye test |
| **Obesity** | All-day sitting + junk | Walk/stairs, stand breaks, healthy tiffin, no sugary drinks |
| **Stress disorders** | Overload, conflicts | Balanced workload, counselling, sleep, hobbies, appreciation |
| **Laptop injuries** | Hunch + tiny keyboard | Stand + external KB/mouse, same 50–70 cm rule |
| **Sleep problems** | Late-night screens, caffeine | No screens 1 hr before bed, fixed sleep time, dark cool room |

**7. Health & safety requirements for computer workplace:** Stable ergonomics furniture • Licensed software + antivirus • UPS/stabiliser • Fire extinguisher + first-aid • Data backup schedule • Displayed emergency numbers • Trained staff + induction for newcomers.

**8. Cautions while working on computer (⚡ often asked):** DON'T open SMPS/monitor/CPU power parts (high voltage even after off) • Unplug before servicing • Dry hands only • Liquids away • One heavy device per socket + MCB known • Use UPS • Antivirus + updates • Proper shutdown (Start → Shut down, not power button) • Cover + lock room after hours.

## 🧠 Tricks
- Numbers: **"50-70 cm, 20-20-20, 90° knees"** — chant thrice.
- 20-20-20 = **Every 20 min → 20 feet away → 20 seconds.**

## ❓ Exam Q&A
**1-mark:** “Monitor distance?” → 50–70 cm. “20-20-20 prevents?” → Eye strain. “Feet position?” → Flat on floor/footrest.
**2-mark:** *Two ergonomic sitting tips.* → Back-supported chair + feet flat; monitor eye-level 50–70 cm + keyboard at elbow height.
**4-mark:** *Case: Riya, 8-hr computer user, eye + back pain. Suggest 4 measures.* → ergonomics + 20-20-20 + breaks/stretch + brightness/clean + water/posture (any 4 detailed).

# Chapter 15: Prevent Accidents and Emergencies

## 🎯 Marks lens
3–4 marks. 5 response rules + accident handling + evacuation + fire/electrical = the finale 4-markers.

## 📖 Deep concepts

**1. Accident vs Emergency:**
| Accident | Emergency |
|---|---|
| Sudden unplanned event causing injury/damage | Serious situation needing IMMEDIATE action |
| Fall, cut, shock, spill | Fire, earthquake, blackout, medical collapse, gas leak |
| Handle + report + record | Alarm + evacuate + call services + count heads |

**2. FIVE golden rules when either strikes (number these in answer!):**
1. **Notice & correctly identify** (what? where? who hurt? fire or shock?)
2. **Get help promptly, suitably** (shout, press alarm, call supervisor; Fire **101**, Ambulance **102/108**, Police 100)
3. **Follow company policy** to prevent FURTHER injury while waiting (switch off machine, cordon area, don't crowd)
4. **Act only within your limits** (no heroics untrained — e.g. don't enter smoke room)
5. **Obey seniors + emergency services** instantly (their word > curiosity)

**3. Types of accidents (5 + 1-line cause):**
- **Trip & Fall** – clutter/uneven floor/cables → clear paths, tape wires
- **Slip & Fall** – wet/oily/polished floor → dry + “Wet Floor” sign + anti-skid mats
- **Escalator/Elevator** – trapped dupatta/laces, sudden jerk, overcrowding → stand clear of edges, hold rail, don't overload lift
- **Falling goods** – unstable stacking, overloaded shelf → heavy-low/light-high, stack limits, no climbing shelves
- **Moving objects** – trolleys, swinging doors, sports balls → walkways marked, door mirrors, speed care

**4. Handling accidents (3 steps + details):**
1. **Attend injured immediately** – reassure, stop bleeding (clean pressure), DON'T move spinal/neck injury (wait for stretcher), keep airway clear.
2. **Inform supervisor** – who/what/where + call first-aider/ambulance with exact location.
3. **Assist supervisor** – fetch kit, guide ambulance, note witnesses, fill incident report (date/time/cause/action) for prevention.

**5. Types of emergencies (3):** **First Aid** (medical: faint, cut, burn, fracture) • **Electrical safety** (shock, sparking, electrical fire) • **Evacuation** (fire, quake, gas leak, threat).

**6. General evacuation procedure (7 steps — learn in order!):**
1. Stay CALM; raise ALARM (shout + press fire alarm)
2. Switch off equipment/power IF safe (don't waste time)
3. Leave by NEAREST SAFE EXIT following green signs; **NEVER use lift in fire**
4. Walk fast, don't run/push; help elderly/disabled; carry nothing heavy
5. Assemble at **assembly point** (open ground, pre-decided)
6. **Headcount/roll call**; report missing to marshal (don't re-enter to search!)
7. Re-enter ONLY when authorities declare safe. *(Practise via MOCK DRILLS; evacuation PLAN MAP on every floor.)*

**7. Fire basics:**
- **Hazards:** overloaded circuits, short-circuit sparks, paper/chemical fuel, kitchen/gas leaks, smoking, blocked exits, dust in panels.
- **Prevention:** no overloading + MCBs, fuel away from heat, gas off after use, alarms + detectors working, exits clear, audits + training, “No Smoking” zones.
- **Fire triangle:** **Fuel + Heat (ignition) + Oxygen** — remove ANY one side: starve fuel (close gas), cool heat (water*), smother oxygen (blanket/CO₂). *(*Never water on electrical/oil fire!)*
- **Material vs Ignition sources — identify & SEPARATE:** Materials (paper, oil, LPG, petrol, wood, cloth) ↔ Ignition (sparks, open flame, heater, short-circuit, static). Store fuels in ventilated metal cabinets away from panels/kitchen.
- **Extinguisher use — PASS:** **P**ull pin → **A**im at BASE of fire → **S**queeze handle → **S**weep side-to-side. Types: Water (paper/wood only), CO₂/Dry Powder (electrical/oil), Foam (oil), Blanket (kitchen/person).
- **If clothes catch fire:** STOP-DROP-ROLL + smother with blanket; cool burns under water 10 min.

**8. First aid for ELECTRICAL emergencies (order is life-or-death!):**
1. **DON'T touch victim** with bare hands (you'll get shocked too)
2. **Switch OFF power** (MCB/main switch) FIRST
3. Separate victim from wire with DRY non-conductor (wooden stick, rubber, dry cloth) while standing on dry insulation (rubber shoes/mat)
4. Call help (supervisor + 102/108)
5. If trained: check breathing/pulse → CPR/recovery position if needed; treat burns with clean DRY cloth (no ointment/ice); keep victim warm + still
6. Shift to hospital even if “feels fine” (delayed heart effects!)

**9. Electrical rescue techniques (toolkit):** Know MAIN switch/MCB locations (labelled!) • Insulate self (rubber gloves/shoes, dry floor) • Use non-conductors only • NEVER water on electrical fire (use CO₂/dry powder) • Test “dead” with tester before touching • Report + barricade damaged points • Annual wiring audit + earthing check.

## 🧠 Tricks
- 5 rules: **"I-Help-Policy-Limits-Obey"** (Identify, Help, Policy, Limits, Obey).
- Evacuation: **"CALm-Switch-Exit-Assemble-Count-Wait"** (Calm, Alarm, Switch-off, Exit-no-lift, Assemble, Count, Wait-for-clearance).
- Fire triangle: **F-H-O** (“FoHO” — Fuel-Heat-Oxygen). PASS = Pull-Aim-Squeeze-Sweep.
- Electrical: **"Don't-touch, Switch-off, Stick-separate, Send-for-help."**

## ⚠️ Mistakes
- Writing “use lift for fast evacuation” — NEVER in fire (power cut traps + shaft spreads smoke).
- “Throw water on electrical fire” — deadly; CO₂/dry powder only.
- Touching shock victim first — always power OFF first.
- Forgetting helpline numbers (101/102/108) in emergency answers.
