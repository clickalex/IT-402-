## Paper 1 — SQP 2025-26 (do this FIRST ⭐)

- **Blueprint:** Part A — U1 (1+1), U2 (2+1), U3 (1+1), U4 (1+1), U5 (1+1). Part B —
  U1 (6 obj + 1 short + 1 long), U2 (6+2+1), U3 (6+1+2), U4 (6+2+1).
- **Style:** Case-based MCQs (Aisha, Ria, Ritika…), one Assertion-Reason (macro function),
  SQL-free DBMS (concepts + RI), safety case-studies (electrical rescue, fire classes).
- **Hot topics:** Fill Format, ToC-empty reason, Templates (Ctrl+Shift+N), Goal Seek EMI,
  Consolidate+link, 1:N vs M:N, RI + cascades, evacuation order, fire classes.

## Paper 2 — SQP 2024-25 (do SECOND)

- **Blueprint:** Same as 2025-26 (identical structure — CBSE kept the pattern).
- **Style:** Direct + case-based mix; macro code details (Sub/End Sub, IDE, valid names);
  forms/reports; fire-prevention plan as a 4-marker.
- **Hot topics:** Fill Format again, 10 heading levels, Scenarios, Subtotal, Relative link,
  RI definition, PK facts, OOS, safety vs security, Track Changes case study.

## Paper 3 — SQP 2023-24 (Units 1–3 + Part A only ⚠️)

- **Blueprint:** Same structure, BUT Unit 4 was **“Web Applications and Security”**
  (blogs, browsers, networks, passwords) — replaced in your syllabus by
  **Health/Safety/Security**. Skip its Unit 4 questions (marked ⛔ below).
- **Still gold for:** Anchoring, ToC characteristics, Goal Seek vs Solver, Scenarios,
  PK vs FK, RI importance, SQL SELECT practice, entrepreneurship myths, antivirus sense.

## Paper 4 — SQP 2022-23 (Units 1–3 + Part A only ⚠️)

- **Blueprint:** Same structure; Unit 4 was **Web Applications** (skip ⛔). Part A
  blueprint that year tested only U2, U3, U4 — but your paper can test all five.
- **Still gold for:** Templates purpose, macros operations, hyperlink types, datatypes,
  Scenarios/GoalSeek/Solver/Subtotal notes, SQL table questions, DBMS intro case study,
  myths about entrepreneurship, stress agents.
